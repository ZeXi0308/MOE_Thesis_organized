The CSV numerator counts singleton token-expert assignments; its denominator counts all native routed slots.
Each per-layer ratio pools the complete generated decode trajectory; layers and tokens are not independent system repetitions.
Uniform iid routing is an analytical reference only. These summaries do not contain complete per-expert inclusion marginals, so there is no fitted skew curve and no separation of routing skew from within-batch correlation.
Compared with a short route trace, this measurement covers later states of long native-generated fixed-length continuations. Different prefix lengths, observation lengths, and content trajectories prevent a causal comparison based on trace duration alone.
A future FP8 run with the same external prefix/output protocol is comparable as a format-specific generated-trajectory observation; it does not hold content/routing fixed or isolate the direct causal effect of quantization.
The benchmark ignores EOS. It is not natural completion, and singleton opportunity alone is not observed policy savings, DRAM traffic, or latency.
