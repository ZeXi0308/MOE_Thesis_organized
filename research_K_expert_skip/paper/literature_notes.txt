Focused literature check -- 2026-10-16 (Asia/Shanghai)

AdapMoE (ICCAD '24) is not just a gate threshold: Eq. (8) combines squared dropped gate mass with layer Fisher-diagonal sensitivity; its full system also includes prefetching and cache allocation. NAEE (ACL 2024) skips the second Mixtral expert when its gate weight is below a calibrated multiple of the first and renormalizes; the official implementation also removes its dispatch mask. Thus a generic batch-independent gate baseline is a component adaptation, not a full reproduction of either system. Ada-K (ICLR 2025) learns a per-token expert-count allocator with PPO. MoE++ (ICLR 2025) trains routing among FFN and zero/copy/constant experts. DynMoE (ICLR 2025) learns top-any thresholds and adapts expert inventory during training. Expert Choice (NeurIPS 2022) selects tokens per expert with fixed capacities; it is already batch-coupled, although its action is not singleton removal.

A material direct neighbor found during this check is SERE (ICLR 2026). It retains the union of top-S primary experts and redirects secondary assignments to similar primary experts, preserving dissimilar critical experts. It explicitly addresses batched decode and has a vLLM implementation. Therefore batch-aware skipping or reducing the expert union cannot be claimed as new. The proposed scoped difference is direct dropping only when the current execution-batch count is one, under rank/gate/mass limits, without a similarity matrix or expert substitution. This is an action-level distinction, not proof of novelty or superiority. Continue the existing mechanism and compare the narrower action space and decision cost; no new research route is introduced.

Writing constraints carried into main.tex:
- q_e is token inclusion probability, with sum q_e = k. If p_e is normalized slot frequency, q_e = k p_e; router softmax is not inclusion probability.
- Counts from correlated natural decode batches need not follow independent Bernoulli occupancy. Replay is labeled separately.
- Sum of initial marginal costs is exact for singleton-only drops; shared-expert baseline drops can jointly remove an expert, so compare exact before/after unions.
- Logical distinct weight bytes use real tensor sizes but are not hardware DRAM transactions.
- F3 with savings on x and quality loss on y improves toward lower right; raw accuracy improves toward upper right.
- Pending empirical cells are not zeros and carry no asserted gains.
