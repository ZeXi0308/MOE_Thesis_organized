not-yet-copied C prefixes. It retains persistent/shm20GiB, rootfs10GiB, persistent
experiment output2GiB, overhead256MiB on every filesystem, and persistent C future
append capacity >=17,424,291,144 bytes (or larger if currently required). No reserve
relaxation option exists. Current free is refreshed after source hashing, before
copy/download, periodically during transfer, and before READY. Guard failures save
the last measured budget in the new attempt receipt.
