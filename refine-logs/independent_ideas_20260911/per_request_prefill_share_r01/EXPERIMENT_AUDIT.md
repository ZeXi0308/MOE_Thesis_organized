## Permissible claim ceiling

The evidence supports only:

> In two order-reversed, single-GPU, 16-request mixed episodes, vLLM's native 512-token long-prefill threshold was read on the executed scheduler path, clipped actual long-prefill allocations, and enabled three observed same-step short first admissions per block while all existing decodes advanced. Relative to native1024, it did not improve complete-request latency or episode wall in either block; therefore this pilot does not justify continuing threshold tuning or claiming a method gain.

Do not claim task/output quality, output equivalence, causal millisecond recovery for any individual clipped event, statistical significance, general throughput/latency regression, dynamic control, action-level novelty, production HTTP behavior, multi-GPU/EP behavior, or an Oracle/headroom result. The report's existing `DESCRIPTIVE_MEASUREMENT_ONLY` label is appropriate ([analysis/TABLE.md:3](/Users/leandrozhao/Desktop/、++++++++/refine-logs/independent_ideas_20260911/per_request_prefill_share_r01/analysis/TABLE.md:3), [DECISIONS.md:61](/Users/leandrozhao/Desktop/、++++++++/refine-logs/independent_ideas_20260911/per_request_prefill_share_r01/DECISIONS.md:61)).
