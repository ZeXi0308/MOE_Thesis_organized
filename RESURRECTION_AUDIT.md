## 6. Claim Boundary

### Now supported

- In this one custom runtime, shared-pre-state batch execution creates repeat-stable numerical trajectories that can change MoE route membership.
- A/C first differs at layer-0 attention output in 6/6 selected events; C/D first differs at MoE output in 6/6.
- Width alone explains 2 route-flip layers in 2/6 events; C/D changes route membership in 2/6 events and 11 layers total.
- Near-boundary association is descriptive: 13/18 A/C flips meet the frozen 0.01 margin; the 18/18 lost/gained-expert ordering crossings follow from the route-flip definition and are not independent amplification evidence.
- Perturbations persist in logits/routes for two later isolated steps, while greedy tokens remain unchanged in 0/8 comparisons.
- Route Capacity observational remains `OPEN`; its causal action remains `UNRUN_SYSTEM_GATE / PAUSED`.

### Not supported

- That the exact C/D cause is expert microbatch grouping rather than sorting, kernel choice, accumulation, combine order, or another MoE implementation detail.
- That all six historical serial routes were exactly reproduced, or that the original capture provenance can be replayed from the retained workspace; only the batched source closure and fresh A/B/C/D outputs are closed locally.
- A population incidence or causal steady-versus-bursty effect; the captures use different request/document sets.
- Native vLLM/SGLang behavior, cross-model behavior, semantic quality harm, request latency, TPOT/P99, SLO-goodput, EP/NCCL, capacity, or controller value.
- Novelty of generic batch nondeterminism, route amplification, an execution-conformance contract, or verify/repair. The strongest direct collision is [From Expert Reduction to Behavioral Divergence](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28097); generic collisions include [vLLM Batch Invariance](https://docs.vllm.ai/en/stable/features/batch_invariance/), [LLM-42](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.17768), and [MarginGate](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.30218).

### Upgrade conditions

1. Native runtime reproduces the same two-event effect from matched target state.
2. Per-expert `M`/token order, kernel choice, expert output, and combine output identify the exact MoE operator.
3. A second model/runtime repeats the mechanism before any generic measurement claim.
4. Semantic quality or request-level claims require their own denominator and controlled workload.
5. A method claim additionally requires a real action, strongest simple/prior-art baselines, Oracle headroom, and full execution cost.

## 8. Direct Answer

**Yes.** The repository contains high-value directions that risked premature death because the decisive experiment was invalid, the accounting was wrong, the operating regime lacked the required action space, a system Gate never ran, or a formulation-level failure was expanded to a family. The clearest cases are legacy Verify H2 (`INVALID_EXPERIMENT`), old Additive-KL 3.77x (`INVALID_EXPERIMENT`), QuotaEP C/RCBA/BCRD/DEPA/Route Capacity action (`UNRUN_SYSTEM_GATE`), and StableBatch/SemanticFence/ErrorToken/resident Prefetch (`PHENOMENON_ALIVE_MECHANISM_DEAD`).

The highest-priority review was batch-dependent route source localization because it could correct both the historical conformance interpretation and Route Capacity's status with one short causal chain. That experiment has now produced a real, repeat-stable **Primary signal**: mixed attention-path and MoE-output divergence in one custom runtime. It does **not** resurrect Route Capacity or any controller. After the direct prior-art collision, the only thesis-relevant residual is whether natural companion identity changes a target through hidden per-expert physical execution in a native runtime. The mechanical next action is the single native internal-mechanism experiment above; until it passes, the result remains `MEASUREMENT_ONLY` and formal method GO remains zero.
